# GOOSEAGEDDON (Goose Patrol) — Credits and Licences

GOOSEAGEDDON was developed by **Cohort 4, Team 8** for the University of York's Engineering 1 (ENG1) project (2026–27).

## Development team

Abdullahi Alhassan, Aidan Wong, Chaoke Du, Harry Challinor, Jaki Sun and Kathryn Moon.

## Technology

- **Java 17** — programming language.
- **libGDX 1.14.2** — 2D game framework; [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
- **LWJGL 3** — desktop backend; [BSD 3-Clause License](https://www.lwjgl.org/guide#license).
- **Gradle** — project build and dependency management.

## Graphics and generated assets

The team used **OpenAI ChatGPT** in creating and refining gameplay graphics, including maps, menu graphics, the student and goose sprites, inventory-item images and seed effects, as described in the Assessment 1 implementation report. Additional gameplay sound effects were also AI-generated. Team members selected, integrated and adapted these resources for the game.
